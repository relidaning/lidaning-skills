### Concepts explained

**The order of operations** — emergency fund (3–6 months expenses, in 货币基金/
存款, not stocks) → pay off high-interest debt (credit cards ~18%, 花呗/借呗
15-20%+) → invest the rest. Debt costing more than expected returns is a
guaranteed daily loss; the emergency fund is what stops a normal market dip
from becoming a forced sale at the bottom.

**Net-worth snapshot** — assets minus liabilities, tracked monthly. Grounds
"how much can I actually invest" in reality and catches lifestyle creep.
